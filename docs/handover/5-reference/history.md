How the portal got to be what it is, in the order it happened, from the first commit on 7 September 2026 to 11 November 2026. It is written from `CHANGELOG.md` and the decision records, which hold the detail, and from the tracker, which holds the reason for each change. This page is the shape of it, and what each stretch of work was for.

## The shape

The portal was built in six stretches. September built the thing and decided its limits. Early October hardened it and added the report files. Mid October was audits, the mentor's requests and a reorganisation. Late October gave it a new look, then HCML's own Zabbix, and then a round of work on speed. The last days so far wrote this handbook, then audited the cache, the live stream, the watcher and sign-out, and fixed what they found.

## What each stretch was for

- September answered what the portal is. A read-only layer, with no database of its own, that works out what Zabbix has no table for: sites, WAN links, a monthly SLA. Every later choice follows from those.
- Early October made it safe to put on a network: a session cookie, hashed passwords, read-only containers, request ids, and files for the reports.
- Mid October is where the audits changed the portal most. Each audit read the earlier claims against the code, and the rows of the tracker are what it found. [The tracker](../../../tracker.md) lists each row with its reason.
- Late October made it look finished, and then made it real: from a frozen copy of Zabbix to HCML's live one. The first real data was slow, and the second half of that week was spent on reading less and keeping more.

## What the history says to a newcomer

- A change here is usually a response to something that was measured or something that broke, and the record says which. The traps page is the same history, told as warnings.
- The data the portal shows was a frozen copy of Zabbix until 29 October. Records written before then, such as the decision records and the dated parts of the guide, describe that copy.
- The two portals were one source tree from 18 September. They have been kept so since, and the parity check is the proof.
